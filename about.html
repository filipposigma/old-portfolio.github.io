I'm Filippos Saltsidis, a London-based UX/UI designer with more than fifteen years of experience in graphic, brand and digital design. I started in print and packaging, ran my own studio, and moved steadily into websites, interfaces and design systems, working with clients and teams in Greece, Berlin and London.

I work best at the start of a project, when the problem isn't clear yet. Before I open Figma, I try to understand who the product is for, what they're trying to do and where they get stuck. Then I turn that into structure: clear hierarchy, simple flows and components a team can reuse and build.

My background in print and brand shows in the details. Typography, spacing and consistency matter to me, and I design with production in mind. After years of working with developers, marketing teams and clients, I'm used to turning a brief into something that ships.

  • Understand users and define the problem
  • Map flows and structure information
  • Design responsive interfaces and prototypes in Figma
  • Build reusable components and light design systems
  • Hand over to developers with clear specs and states
